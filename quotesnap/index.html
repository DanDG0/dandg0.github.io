Questions

Good to know

Do I need to create an account?

Yes, it takes a minute: your name, email or phone, and a password, so only you can open your quotes and invoices. Your team members each get an invite code and choose their own password. Your data is saved on this device; use “Download backup” in Settings to move it.

Does QuoteSnap read my WhatsApp?

Only what you paste in. The chat is read right on your device and never uploaded anywhere.

Will it send messages for me?

QuoteSnap writes the message and opens WhatsApp with it ready to go. You tap send, so nothing goes out without you seeing it.

What if it reads a price wrong?

Every line is editable before you issue the document. If it picked the wrong person as “you”, switch it under Original chat and it re-reads everything.

Does it work in my country?

Yes, anywhere WhatsApp works. QuoteSnap picks up your country and sets your currency, number style, phone code, date format and tax name (VAT, GST, sales tax…). It reads prices however people type them: $350, €350, 350 €, £1,200, ₹1,20,000, 1.200,50 €, 350 kr, ¥5,000 and more. You can change any of it in Settings, and bill a client in a different currency job by job.

Can my employees use it too?

Yes, with the Teams add-on ($3/month, priced for your country). Add your people, give each a role, and they only see what their job needs. Field staff can see their assigned jobs without any prices, while Finance handles quotes and invoices. You also choose who is in each team chat.

Can I save it as a PDF?

Yes. Tap Print / PDF and choose “Save as PDF”. Only the document prints, not the app.
